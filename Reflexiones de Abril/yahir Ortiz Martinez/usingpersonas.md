<h2>Using Personas</h2>
        <h3>Yahir Ortiz Martinez - 04/30/2025</h3>
        <p>
            In the reflections on personas, specifically the secretary who manages clinical files, the project shows how knowledge about their needs and workflow directly influenced the user interface of the prototype, the design and strategic placement of the "File" and "Search File" buttons. During the Persona research phase, it was identified that the secretary spends a significant part of her time searching for files for coordinators or therapists. With this knowledge, in the prototype it was decided to place these two buttons prominently and easily accessible on the main screen, perhaps with visually distinctive icons and clear labels.
        </p>
        <p>
            But it was necessary to understand how she used the files to be able to centralize tasks and objectives.
        </p>
