### 2. Reflection: Using Personas

In our first work with Personas, we got valuable insights about the user roles and needs. This reflection shows how we applied that information in the development of the prototype.

**Implementation example:**  
One of our key personas was a psychologist with a master's degree, who talked about work overload. From this person, we learned that reducing steps in repetitive tasks was very important. Based on this, we designed a quick access panel on the main screen, where users can filter patients by the kind of treatment they are recieving, or even search them by name.

**Final reflection:**  
This funcionality comes directly from the need of eficiency under time pressure. By translating these needs into concrete UI elements, the prototype not only reflects real workflows, but also shows how well-defined personas guide user-centered design. This process validated that making personas early is not just theorical, but very important for practical desitions in design.
