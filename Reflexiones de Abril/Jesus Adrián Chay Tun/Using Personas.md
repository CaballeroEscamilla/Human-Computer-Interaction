### 2. Reflection: Using Personas

The personas technique was used to understand the profiles and needs of the system users. This was a very important part for choosing and creating the parts of the system.

The clearest example I can give is the file digitization part. This idea came from the profile of one of the clinic’s therapists. In this profile, it said that the clinic still uses very simple tools (like pen and paper), and these papers are kept in file drawers. Because of this, the user thought it was important to improve the physical and digital tools in the clinic. The mockup is a full example of this change, where the therapist’s ideas about digital tools are now shown in a digital system for the clinic. This is a first step to digitization. It helps with therapist management, appointment schedules, and also with the things therapists give to their supervisors. This is the first step in the digital process for the clinic. The goal is to create a system to manage appointments, therapists, appointment requests, and to digitize and scan records, including tools for first contact with new patients.

Finally, the personas technique was a very useful tool in the process of making the prototype. It helped to understand the needs, problems, and suggestions of possible users. This made it easier to plan and prepare the important functions of the system.
